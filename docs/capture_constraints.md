# CUDA Graph capture constraints and graph-invalidation catalogue — v1 (S2)

Scope: SD1.5 + LCM-LoRA (fused, fp16, 4 steps), diffusers 0.35.2, torch 2.9.1+cu126, RTX 4090.
Entries 1–8 and 16 are v0 (S1) observations from building the MVP arms (E0/E1/G1/G2/C1/C2/C1+G/C5/C6).
Entries 9–15 are v1 (S2) controlled perturbations: each has a minimal reproduction in `tests/test_invalidation.py`
(toy modules; entries 14 and 15 also confirmed once on the real fp16 UNet), observations in `results/S2/invalidation/<NN>.json`,
torch 2.9.1+cu126, RTX 4090. Entry 13 is deferred.
